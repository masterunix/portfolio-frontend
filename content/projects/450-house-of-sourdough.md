Client work for 450 House of Sourdough, an artisanal bakery in Jaipur: the storefront, plus a Flask and SQLite system for orders, subscriptions and B2B accounts.

As the bakery's IT consultant (June – November 2025), I built and deployed its web platform, with online orders, payments and inventory, which made ordering faster and gave the business data on what sells.
